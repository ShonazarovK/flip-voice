## 3. Description

FlipVoice is your all-in-one offline audio laboratory for reversing sounds, exploring backward talk, shifting pitch, and playing with audio playback speed.

Whether you want to solve viral backward singing challenges, create surreal sound design effects, or discover what your voice sounds like in reverse, FlipVoice gives you high-fidelity sound manipulation directly on your iPhone and iPad.

KEY FEATURES:
• Frame-Accurate Audio Reversal: Invert any sound with sample-exact precision.
• Independent Pitch & Speed Controls: Tune pitch across -24 to +24 semitones and speed from 0.5x to 2.0x in real-time.
• Backwards Talk Challenge: Record a phrase, listen to its backwards phonetics, record your imitation, and flip it back to see if you can speak backward!
• Interactive Waveform & Trimmer: Visualize audio, set custom start/end points, and apply smooth fade-ins and fade-outs.
• Universal Import: Import WAV, MP3, M4A, AAC, AIFF, and extract audio tracks from videos directly from your Files app.
• Flexible Export & Sharing: Export in high-quality M4A (AAC) or uncompressed WAV to Messages, AirDrop, or your Files app.
• 100% Private & Offline: Everything runs directly on your device. Zero cloud uploads, zero account creation, and zero tracking.

## 5. App Review Notes (Instructions for Apple Reviewer)
```
Welcome Apple App Review Team!

FlipVoice is an offline audio utility and entertainment app for recording and manipulating sound.

To test the core features:
1. Studio Tab: Tap the red Record button to record a brief audio sample (microphone permission prompt will appear). Tap Finish & Edit to immediately open it in the Audio Lab. Alternatively, tap 'Import Audio File' to select any sample audio file from the Files app.
2. Audio Lab Tab: 
   - Tap 'Reverse' to invert playback.
   - Adjust the 'Speed' and 'Pitch Shift' sliders to hear real-time pitch shifting powered by AVAudioUnitTimePitch.
   - Use the waveform handles to trim the audio and adjust Fade In / Fade Out sliders.
   - Tap 'Export & Share' to render and share via the native iOS share sheet.
3. Challenge Tab:
   - Follow the 4-step interactive 'Backwards Talk Challenge'. Record a phrase in Step 1, play it reversed in Step 2, record an imitation in Step 3, and flip it back in Step 4 to compare results side-by-side.
4. Library Tab: View saved recordings, swipe left to rename or delete, or tap any entry to load it back into the editor.
5. Settings Tab: Switch between English, Russian, and Uzbek languages, toggle Dark/Light themes, and review the Privacy Policy.

All processing occurs 100% on-device with zero backend dependencies.
```
