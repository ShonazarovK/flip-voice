# Privacy Policy for FlipVoice

**Effective Date:** November 2026

FlipVoice ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage.

## 1. 100% On-Device Processing
FlipVoice operates entirely offline. All audio recordings, audio conversions, reversal transformations, pitch shifts, speed modulations, and waveform trimming are computed strictly on your device using native Apple iOS frameworks (AVFoundation, Accelerate).

## 2. No Data Collection or Transmission
We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, voice recordings, identifiers, or usage telemetry:
- **No Analytics:** FlipVoice contains zero third-party analytics libraries.
- **No Advertising:** No advertising identifiers or ad SDKs are included.
- **No Accounts:** No account registration or login is required.
- **No Cloud Uploads:** No audio files leave your device.

## 3. Device Permissions
- **Microphone Access (`NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`):** Required solely to record your voice or surrounding sounds when you actively tap the record button. Recordings are saved in your local app sandbox.
- **File Sharing (`UIFileSharingEnabled`):** Allows you to view, backup, or export your saved audio files through the native iOS Files app or Mac Finder.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
Your audio files and metadata remain stored on your physical device in the app's local document directory. You can delete individual recordings or clear all app data at any time directly through the in-app Library or by deleting the app.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Because FlipVoice collects zero personal data from any user, it complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar global privacy regulations.
